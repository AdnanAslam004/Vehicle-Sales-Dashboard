# Business Questions

This project answers the following business questions using SQL Server and Grafana.

1. How many vehicles were sold in total?

2. What is the total revenue generated from vehicle sales?

3. What is the average vehicle selling price?

4. Which 10 vehicle brands generated the highest revenue?

5. Which 10 vehicle models had the highest sales volume?

6. Which states sold the most vehicles?

7. What are the most common vehicle body types?

8. What is the distribution of automatic and manual vehicles?

9. Which vehicle brands have the highest average selling price?

10. How does vehicle condition affect the average selling price?

11. Which states generated the highest revenue?

12. Which states have the highest average selling price?

13. How does total revenue vary by manufacturing year?

14. How does vehicle sales volume vary by manufacturing year?

15. Which brands have the highest average vehicle condition?

16. Which brands have the highest average odometer reading?

17. Which vehicle models have the highest average selling price?
